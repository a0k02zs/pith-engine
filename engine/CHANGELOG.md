# Engine contract changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21 (all changes below are inside 0.1.0; nothing is breaking)

First publish. Then these service requests were added the same day:

- **Campaigns (2):** brain read = `GET /clients/{id}/brain`; approval decisions `approved | rejected | changes_requested` adopted; compliance returns `pass | warn | block` (your `fail` = `block`); your events become `svc.campaigns/<noun>.<verb>`; KPIs `roas, cpa, pipeline_value, cost_per_opportunity, incremental_lift, ctr, cvr` exist as named.
- **Email (1):** contact and consent fields match your draft (`consent_bases` adds `double_opt_in`, `corporate_subscriber`, `casl_business_exemption`, `prior_express_written`, `opt_out_regime`); `sender_identity` in the brand profile; `suppressed` + `suppression_reason`; your `email.sent` etc. = `engine/outbound.recorded` with `service=email, type=sent`.
- **Video (3):** likeness and voice consent = `rights-grant.json` (scope `likeness`, `voice_clone`, `avatar`); `media_rules` in the brand profile; "needs-human" = `warn`; KPIs `hook_rate, hold_rate, completion_rate, cost_per_finished_video, days_brief_to_delivery`.
- **Influencer (6):** creators = `party.json` (`kind=creator`, `licences[]` with `uae_media_council` / `mawthooq`); approval kinds `shortlist, contract, content, payout`; rules `G-PAID-DISCLOSURE`, `AE-INFLUENCER-LICENCE`, `SA-INFLUENCER-LICENCE`, `EU-AI-LABEL`; payouts = `engine/outbound.recorded` `type=payout_sent`.
- **Content (7):** kinds unprefixed (`plan`, `copy`, `claim` with `service=content`); roles `native_editor`, `client_marketing_lead`, `external_contact` added; service-only KPIs as `content.<name>`; `geo.citation_share` = `ai_citation_share`; `artifact_kinds` adds blog, whitepaper, ebook, case_study; brand score = `POST /artifacts/{id}/brand-score`, 0 to 100.
- **Search (5):** 12 KPI codes added; role `client_developer`; guardrail G9 (live site changes always need a `go_live` gate); rules `UK-VAT-PRICE`, `EU-OMNIBUS-PRICE`, `GCC-ARABIC-B2C`, `CA-SPECIAL-AD`, `G-AI-IMAGE-LABEL`; brain `organization.same_as[]`.
- **Paid social (11):** 9 KPI codes added; rules `G-LIST-UPLOAD`, `G-SENSITIVE-TARGETING`, `G-MINOR-TARGETING`, `G-POLITICAL`, `G-RESTRICTED-PRODUCT`, `UK-HFSS`, `GCC-ALCOHOL`; `eligible_bases.paid_ad` per market; list uploads use `/audiences/resolve` with `channel=paid_ad, purpose=marketing` (no separate purpose); request fields `paid.targeting`, `paid.political`, `paid.product_category`. Your `ps.*` codes map: pixel → `UK-COOKIE`/`EU-COOKIE`, creator disclosure → `G-PAID-DISCLOSURE`, licence → `AE/SA-INFLUENCER-LICENCE`, AI → `EU-AI-LABEL`/`G-AI-IMAGE-LABEL`, language → `QC-FRENCH`/`GCC-ARABIC-B2C`, claims → `G-CLAIM-APPROVED`, special category → `US/CA-SPECIAL-AD`.
- **Analytics (12):** ownership split recorded in `specs/analytics.md`; KPIs `attributed_revenue, mmm_roi, tracking_coverage` added.
- **Affiliate (15):** payout batch payload `{lines[], totals_by_currency}`; `/audiences/resolve` accepts `party_ids[]` (consents can now belong to a party); KPIs `activation_rate, reversal_rate, payout_amount, partner_revenue_concentration, epc`.
- **Events (16):** 9 KPI codes added; `registered, attended, no_show` event types; channel `in_person_event`.
- **Community (18):** 5 KPI codes added; new tool risk `remove` and guardrail G10: removals need no check or gate.
- **Video (3), second round:** 9 KPI codes added; rules `FR-IMAGE-LABEL`, `QC-FRENCH-MASTER`, `AE-FILM-CONSENT`, `US-FAKE-TESTIMONIAL`; `rights-grant` adds `voice_languages`, `edit_scope`, `fee`, `guardian_evidence_ref`. Rights pattern: check `GET /clients/{id}/rights?party_id=…&active_on=` in a `check` step first; only if a grant is missing, open a gate or `wait` on `engine/rights.changed` with a timeout.
- **Messaging (10):** suppression and opt-out syncs use risk `remove` (G10, never blocked); `services/messaging/market-rules.yaml` is the channel overlay for sms, whatsapp and push in `compliance-rules.json` (SG DNC freshness now 21 days); `/audiences/resolve` returns `send_window`; contact fields `whatsapp_window_open_until` and `push_tokens[]`; posted client events emit `engine/client.event`.
- **Analytics (12), second round:** role `client_marketing_lead` confirmed in enums; `enums.attribution_models` adds `last_non_direct`, `time_decay`, `b2b_account`; 14 KPI codes added.
- **Brand (13):** brand profile adds `messaging`, `boilerplate`, `taglines`, `voice.tone_by_situation`, `voice.style_rules`, `visual.logo_rules`, `visual.templates`, `visual.color_roles`. Brand owns the brand-score rubric per client.
- **Campaigns (2), second round:** service-integration.md item 9: every channel registers `<service>.campaign_assets` and waits for `svc.campaigns/market.launched`.
- **Email (1), second round:** `enums.tool_risk_meaning` and `enums.tool_registry`; ESP list sync = `write_internal`, suppression/pause = `remove`; email overlay `config/email-market-rules.json` linked from compliance-rules.json.
- **Content (7), second round:** new step type `task` for human work (interviews); case study release = a `copy` gate with `external_contact` for the text, plus a `rights_grants` row with scope `case_study_release` (or `testimonial`) for ongoing use and expiry.
- **New mechanism:** `compliance-rules.json` → `service_overlays` lets a service own extra rules for its own work (ABM, PR, paid social, video so far). The gate merges them for that service.
- **PR (14):** party fields `do_not_contact` (global opt-out when `client_id` is null) and `consent_basis`; rule `G-DO-NOT-CONTACT`; compliance request `audience.party_ids[]`; engine actions `client.pause_scheduled` (risk remove) and `client.resume_scheduled` in `enums.engine_actions`; client fields `is_listed`, `exchange`; `pr_lead` decides press and crisis escalations. Citation targets come from the Search thread (`svc.search/*`), not from the engine. `pr.*` KPIs are fine as service-only codes.
- **Social (4):** brand profile `social` block (`autonomy_level`, `pillars`, `accounts`, `time_windows`, `pause_list`) and `native_editor_languages`; role `community_manager`; `social.*` KPIs fine; pausing a scheduled queue is risk `remove` (G10), or call `engine` action `client.pause_scheduled`.
- **ABM (8):** approval kind `handoff` (client_sales); company fields `tier`, `fit_score`, `intent_score`, `engagement_score`, `cluster`, `scores_updated_at`; ABM events stay in the ABM spec (service-owned names need no engine change); ABM rules via `service_overlays.abm`.
- **CRO (17):** rules `G-DARK-PATTERN`, `SG-GST-PRICE`, `GCC-VAT-PRICE`, `UK-DRIP-PRICE`, `CA-DRIP-PRICE`, `QC-TRACKING-OFF`; `UK-COOKIE` and `EU-COOKIE` now name A/B test, heatmap and session-recording tools; `experiment.pause` (all traffic back to control) is risk `remove` (G10); `cro.*` KPIs and `svc.cro/*` events are fine as service-owned names.
- **CRM ops (9):** lifecycle stages `sal`, `nurture`, `disqualified`; approval kind `data_change` (ops_lead, 72 h; only skip: first-party import of 500 rows or fewer); `enums.conversion_kinds` lists purchase, lead, mql, sal, sql, opportunity, closed_won, closed_lost, registration, booking. The engine is the record of consent; CRM mirroring of `engine/consent.changed` as risk `remove` is right.
- **PR (14), second round:** tool `mailbox` in `enums.tool_registry` (action `drafts.create`, risk `write_internal`; a person sends the draft); engine actions `parties.upsert` (write_internal) and `metrics.query` (read), the same as the `/parties` and `/metrics` endpoints; `service_overlays.pr` now points to `services/pr-thought-leadership/config/compliance-overlay.json`.
- **Influencer (6), second round:** engine action `rights.create` (write_internal, the same as `POST /clients/{client_id}/rights`); `parties.upsert` (above) also covers creators saved as `kind=creator`. Action names inside an external tool (`influencer`, `esign`, `payout`) stay service-owned; the engine checks the step's risk, not the action name.
- **Campaigns (2), third round:** engine action `spend_policies.upsert` with risk `spend` (not write_internal): it writes caps that a `budget` or `budget_change` gate approved earlier in the same run (G1), and `set_by` is that approver, because spend_policies are the caps agents cannot change on their own. Paid tool action names (`campaigns.activate`, `budgets.apply_within_policy`, `budgets.apply`) stay service-owned; the step risk decides the gate.
