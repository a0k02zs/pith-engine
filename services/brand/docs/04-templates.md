## 2. Interview guide (45 minutes)

Consent first: "We record this call to take notes. We use your words without your name. Do you agree?"
Record the answer. Stop if the answer is no.

1. Tell me about your role and a normal week. (5 min)
2. Before you used [client], how did you solve [problem]? What did not work? (10 min)
3. What made you start to look for a new solution? What happened that day? (5 min)
4. Which options did you look at? Why did you choose [client]? What nearly stopped you? (10 min)
5. How would you describe [client] to a colleague or friend? (5 min)
6. If [client] were gone tomorrow, what would you miss? (5 min)
7. What is 1 thing they must improve? (5 min)

## 3. Positioning statement

> For **[target]** who **[need or job]**,
> **[brand]** is the **[frame of reference]**
> that **[point of difference]**,
> because **[reason to believe]**.

Test: Can a competitor sign this statement too? If yes, the point of difference is not yet sharp.

## 8. Brand guidelines outline

1. Our story: purpose, positioning, promise
2. Messaging house and boilerplate
3. Voice and tone (with examples per market)
4. Claims rules: how to use the claims register
5. Logo: versions, clear space, minimum size, misuse
6. Colour: values, roles, accessible pairs
7. Typography: families, hierarchy, Arabic and other scripts
8. Imagery and illustration
9. Icons, graphic devices, layout grid
10. Motion and video basics (logo animation, lower-thirds, end cards)
11. Channel examples: web, social, email, ads, slides, documents
12. Market versions: Arabic and RTL, Quebec French, other locales
13. AI use rules: what AI may make, what needs a designer, AI labels
14. Files, templates and contacts

## 9. Brand architecture decision tree

1. Do the products share the same customers and the same promise? Yes → **branded house** (1 master brand).
2. Does a product need its own identity to win (different price level, different audience, risk to the master brand)? Yes → **house of brands** (separate brands).
3. Does the product need its own identity but gains trust from the master brand? Yes → **endorsed brand** ("X, by Master").
4. If not sure, use a branded house. It costs less to market.

## 10. Identity QA checklist

- [ ] Logo: full colour, mono, reversed, icon, horizontal, stacked
- [ ] Arabic and RTL lockup (GCC in scope)
- [ ] Clear space and minimum size defined
- [ ] All text colour pairs pass WCAG 2.2 AA
- [ ] SVG, PDF, PNG at 3 sizes; HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone
- [ ] Font licences for web, desktop and app in all markets
- [ ] Human designer is the author of the final logo; design files kept
- [ ] Figma library published; Canva brand kit set up
- [ ] Templates for social, email, ads, slides, documents, video
