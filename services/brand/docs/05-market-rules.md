# Service 13 · Brand strategy and design · Market rules

Version 1 · 21 Oct 2026. Market codes follow `engine/contracts/enums.json`.
Launch order: UK, SG, NA, GCC, EU (plan, "The 5 target markets").

**Not legal advice.** This is a planning summary. A local trademark lawyer confirms each market
before a client uses a new name, logo or tagline. Rules change; the content and brand lead checks
this file every quarter.

## 1. Rules for all markets

1. **Human authorship of the logo.** In the US, the Copyright Office and the courts require a human author for copyright. The EU test asks for the author's own intellectual creation. The UK has a special rule for computer-generated works, but it is under review. So a human designer makes the final logo and identity. AI makes mood boards and explorations only. Keep the design files as proof.
2. **Trademark first, then launch.** Run a knock-out search before the client sees a name as "recommended". Run a full search and file before launch. Use the Madrid Protocol where the office is a member, and confirm membership with the lawyer for each GCC country.
3. **Font and image licences** must cover every market and every use (web, app, print, broadcast).
4. **Interview data** follows the data law of the person's country. Use the engine consent record. Delete recordings after 12 months unless the client asks for a shorter time.
5. **Accessibility.** Colour pairs meet WCAG 2.2 AA in all markets. This also helps with the EU Accessibility Act for digital products and services sold in the EU from 28 June 2025.

## 2. Per market

### UK (launch 1st)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trademark office | UK IPO. An EU trade mark does not cover the UK. |
| Language | British English spelling. |
| Voice | Direct, low hype; understatement works better than big claims. |
| Claims | CAP Code: objective claims need evidence before publication. Brand claims go into the claims register. |
| Design notes | No special script needs. |

### SG · Singapore (launch 2nd)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trademark office | IPOS (Madrid member). |
| Language | English first. For some B2C audiences: Mandarin (Simplified Chinese), Malay, Tamil. Check the name sounds right in Mandarin and Malay. |
| Voice | Practical, value-led. |
| Culture | Red and gold are positive for Chinese audiences, popular at Chinese New Year. White and black alone can suggest mourning. The number 4 sounds like "death" in Mandarin and Cantonese; 8 is lucky. Respect Malay-Muslim, Indian and Chinese festivals equally. |
| Design notes | Test the logo with Chinese characters if a Chinese name is in scope. |

### NA · United States and Canada (launch 3rd)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trademark offices | USPTO (US), CIPO (Canada). Both are Madrid members. File in both: 1 does not cover the other. |
| Language | US English for the US; Canadian English for Canada. **Quebec (CA-QC): French.** |
| Quebec | Charter of the French Language: marketing in Quebec must be in French. Since 1 June 2025, a trademark in another language can stay on products and signs only if it is registered, and generic or descriptive words in it must appear in French. French must be markedly predominant on outdoor signs. Plan a French tagline and a French version of descriptive words from the start. |
| Voice | Confident, benefit-first, with proof. |
| Claims | FTC: claims need a reasonable basis before use. Canada: Competition Act rules on misleading claims and environmental claims. |
| Design notes | Check that the name and logo also work in French. |

### GCC · UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman (launch 4th)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trademark offices | File in each country: UAE Ministry of Economy, Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP), and the offices of Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. Do not assume 1 filing covers the region. Confirm Madrid coverage for each country with the lawyer. |
| Language | English for most B2B. Gulf Arabic for B2C and government-linked buyers. Arabic is required on product labels and on shop signs in most GCC countries. |
| Arabic name | Give 2 Arabic options: a transliteration (sound) and, if useful, a meaning-based name. A native Gulf Arabic reviewer approves. Check the Arabic name for bad meanings. |
| Logo | Make an Arabic lockup and a right-to-left layout. Use an Arabic font designed to match the Latin font. Do not flip the logo as a mirror image. |
| Culture | Respect religion, culture and public morals. Avoid religious symbols and verses in brand assets. Modest dress in imagery; Saudi Arabia is more conservative. Green has strong links to Islam and to the Saudi flag; use it with care. Avoid pork, alcohol and gambling cues unless the client sells them where it is legal. Avoid the left hand in eating or giving imagery. |
| Voice | Respectful, premium, relationship-first. |
| National brands | Do not use national flags, emblems or rulers' images in brand assets without permission. |

### EU (launch 5th)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trademark office | EUIPO (1 EU trade mark for all 27 countries), or national offices. Check national earlier rights in the lead countries. |
| Language | Start with 1 or 2 lead countries (the Pith pipeline example is the Netherlands). English works for Dutch and Nordic B2B. German, French and Spanish need a native editor. Check the name in each lead language. |
| France | French language law (Loi Toubon): advertising in France must be in French; a foreign tagline needs a French translation. |
| Voice | Factual, precise; avoid US-style hype. |
| AI | EU AI Act Article 50 transparency rules apply from 2 August 2026. Label AI-generated or manipulated images and video shown to the public. Mood boards are internal and need no label. |
| Accessibility | European Accessibility Act: digital products and services sold to EU consumers must be accessible. |
| Data | GDPR for interview recordings and survey data; tell people how long you keep them. |
