# Content marketing — rules for each market

Source: [Pith M Automation Plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/5mM1UebZQizfeEsfa2Ca67), sections "7 · Content marketing" and "The 5 target markets".
Machine-readable copy: `market-rules.json`. Agents read the JSON. People read this file.
This is a planning summary. It is not legal advice. A local lawyer confirms each market before launch.

Launch order: United Kingdom, Singapore, North America, Middle East (UAE and Saudi Arabia first), European Union.

## Rules for all markets

1. Write in the language and spelling of the market. Do not machine-translate a page and publish it. A native editor checks every translated page.
2. Show money in the local currency. Show dates in the local format.
3. Each page has a named expert author and a "last reviewed" date.
4. Label AI-assisted content where the law or the platform asks for it. The EU AI Act Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026.
5. A gated asset form collects only the fields the market allows. It links to the client privacy notice. Consent goes to the engine data layer with the contact.
6. A case study uses a customer name, logo or quote only with a signed release.

## 1 · United Kingdom (launch 1st)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | British English spelling (optimise, colour, organisation). |
| Format | GBP (£). Dates DD/MM/YYYY. |
| Tone | Direct and low-hype. Understatement works better than big claims. Show proof. |
| Topic calendar | January sales, Black Friday, Christmas, Boxing Day. B2B: end of tax year (5 April). |
| Gated forms | UK GDPR and PECR. Consent or soft opt-in before marketing email to individuals. Privacy notice link. |
| Disclosure | CAP Code and ASA: label sponsored content and paid partner content "Ad". |
| Tracking | Cookie consent before analytics on content pages. |

## 2 · Singapore (launch 2nd)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | English with British spelling. For some B2C audiences, add Mandarin, Malay or Tamil versions of key pages. |
| Format | SGD (S$). Dates DD/MM/YYYY. |
| Tone | Practical and value-led. Give clear numbers and steps. |
| Topic calendar | Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Deepavali, 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12 sales, Great Singapore Sale. |
| Gated forms | PDPA 2012: consent and a notified purpose. If you collect phone numbers for marketing, check the Do Not Call Registry before SMS or calls. |
| Disclosure | ASAS Code: disclose paid and sponsored content. |
| Political content | Do not publish political content for clients (Foreign Interference rules). |

## 3 · North America (launch 3rd)

### United States

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | US English spelling (optimize, color). |
| Format | USD ($). Dates MM/DD/YYYY. |
| Tone | Confident and benefit-first, with proof: numbers, case studies, named customers. |
| Topic calendar | Super Bowl, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Monday. B2B: Q4 budget season. |
| Gated forms | Honour opt-outs and Global Privacy Control in states that need it (CCPA/CPRA and others). |
| Disclosure | FTC Endorsement Guides. FTC fake reviews rule: no invented testimonials or quotes in case studies. |

### Canada

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | Canadian English spelling (colour, centre, but "organization" is common). French is required for Quebec. A native French editor writes or checks every Quebec page. |
| Format | CAD (C$). Dates: use YYYY-MM-DD or write the month as a word to avoid confusion. |
| Topic calendar | Canada Day (1 July), Labour Day, Canadian Thanksgiving (2nd Monday of October), Boxing Day. |
| Gated forms | CASL: express consent before the first marketing email. Record the consent. PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 for personal data. |
| Disclosure | Ad Standards disclosure guidelines. |

## 4 · Middle East — GCC (launch 4th)

Start with the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Then add Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman.

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | English for most B2B. Gulf Arabic versions of key pages for B2C and government-linked buyers. Arabic pages use right-to-left layouts. A native Arabic editor checks every Arabic page. |
| Format | Local currency: AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR. Weekends: Saturday and Sunday in the UAE; Friday and Saturday in the other 5. Do not publish major pieces on the local weekend. |
| Tone | Respectful, premium and relationship-first. Saudi Arabia is more conservative. |
| Topic calendar | Ramadan and Eid (dates move each year). National days: UAE 2 December, Saudi 23 September, Saudi Founding Day 22 February, Qatar 18 December, Kuwait 25 February. Dubai Shopping Festival, White Friday. B2B events: GITEX, LEAP. |
| Sensitive topics | Respect religion, culture and public morals. No content on politics, religion or alcohol unless the client brain allows it and a person approves. Images: modest dress, no religious symbols used as decoration. |
| Gated forms | Follow each country's data law (UAE PDPL, Saudi PDPL, Qatar PDPPL, Bahrain PDPL, Oman PDPL, Kuwait CITRA rules). Saudi Arabia: check data transfer rules before form data leaves the country. |

## 5 · European Union (launch 5th)

Start with 1 or 2 lead countries. The Pith pipeline uses the Netherlands as the EU example.

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Language | English is acceptable for Dutch and Nordic B2B. German, French and Spanish need full writing or translation by a native editor. |
| Format | EUR (€). Dates DD/MM/YYYY or DD.MM.YYYY (German). |
| Tone | Factual and precise. No US-style hype. |
| Gated forms | GDPR: lawful basis, privacy notice, data subject rights. Germany: double opt-in for email. If contact data comes from a third party, the engine sends an Article 14 notice. |
| Disclosure | Unfair Commercial Practices Directive and national laws (for example the French influencer law of 2023). |
| AI labels | EU AI Act Article 50: label AI-generated or AI-manipulated content where the article applies (from 2 August 2026). |
| Tracking | Cookie consent before any tracking. |
