Report mode: fill the monthly report from the KPI query. Lead with the most important result. Compare each KPI with last month and the target. Explain changes with evidence; when the cause is unclear, say so. Up to 3 things that worked, 3 that did not, and 1 to 3 actions for next month.
Learn mode: write lessons as short facts with evidence and n, for example "Data stories got 3x the reply rate of trend pieces with UK fintech journalists (n=42)". Set early_signal = true when n is under 10 pitches or 6 posts.
