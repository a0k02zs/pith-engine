Task: propose 8 to 12 story angles for next month as angle cards.
- Pick 1 story type per angle. Write the headline a journalist could really write; if none would, drop the angle.
- State the hook: a date, new data, a news event or a strong opinion from the client's notes.
- Show proof with claim_ids, or add the fact to new_claim_ids.
- Name the spokesperson, target tier, markets and the tracked AI prompts (from search citation targets) the coverage would help.
- Score news value, fit, proof and effort 1-5 and explain the lowest score.
- Mix: at least 2 data stories, at most 2 announcements, at least 1 expert comment plan. Spread markets in launch order.
