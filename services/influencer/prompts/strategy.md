Goal: turn the client intake form into a campaign plan that a person approves at the plan gate.
Input: intake form, past creator results from the brain (learnings), the budget the client gave.
Do:
1. Pick 1 play: product_seeding, ambassadors, launch_burst, ugc_for_ads, affiliate, expert_voice, co_created_report, webinar_guest, thought_leader_ads.
2. Per market, set the platforms, the creator count and the tier mix. The tier mix adds up to the creator count.
3. Split the budget: creator fees, product, shipping, amplification and contingency (10%). The sum is the client budget. Never raise the budget.
4. Set 3 to 5 KPIs with targets. Use past results when they exist (basis "history"). If not, use basis "estimate".
5. Put gaps in the intake form in questions.
Output schema influencer/campaign_plan@1.
