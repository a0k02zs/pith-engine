# Influencer marketing playbook

## 1. What the client buys

Pith M finds creators whose audience matches the client, runs fair deals at scale, makes sure every post is legal in its market, measures the sales, and reuses the best content in ads.

3 packages:

| Package | For | Scope per month | Typical length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed | B2C brands that test creators | 10 to 30 nano and micro creators, product gifting plus small fees, 1 market | 3 months |
| Scale | B2C brands with proof | 20 to 60 creators across tiers, always-on, paid amplification, up to 3 markets | 6 to 12 months |
| Expert | B2B clients | 3 to 10 industry experts and LinkedIn creators, webinars, reports, podcast spots | 6 months |

Pith M fee model: monthly management fee plus a pass-through creator budget. Pith M does not mark up creator fees. This keeps the deal fair for creators and transparent for clients.

## 2. Creator tiers

Follower counts are a guide. Engagement quality and audience match matter more than size.

| Tier | Followers | Best use | Deal type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1k to 10k | Trust, UGC for ads, local reach | Gifting, small fee, affiliate |
| Micro | 10k to 100k | Niche conversion, the main volume tier | Fee plus affiliate |
| Mid | 100k to 500k | Reach in 1 market or 1 niche | Fee, usage rights |
| Macro | 500k to 1M | Launch reach | Fee, usage rights, exclusivity |
| Mega and celebrity | 1M+ | Brand campaigns | Agency-negotiated, legal review |
| B2B expert | Any size | Credibility with buyers | Fee per asset, co-created content |

Default mix for a Scale client: 60% of the creators are nano and micro, 30% mid, 10% macro. Change the mix from results after month 2.

## 3. Plays

### B2C plays
1. **Product seeding.** Gift product to 50 to 200 nano creators. No post is required. A creator who posts about a gift must still disclose it as a gift in all 5 markets (for example "Gifted" in the UK, a material connection under the US FTC Guides). The brief says this. Use it to find the creators who like the product.
2. **Always-on ambassadors.** 10 to 30 creators on 3 to 6 month deals, 1 to 2 posts per month each, a personal code each.
3. **Launch burst.** 20 to 50 creators post in the same 7-day window, joined to a paid campaign (Service 2).
4. **UGC for ads.** Creators make content for the client's ad accounts. The creator does not post it. Fee covers usage rights.
5. **Affiliate creators.** Commission only, run with Service 15.

### B2B plays
1. **Expert voice.** Industry experts post about a problem the client solves, on LinkedIn, with the client tagged as a paid partner.
2. **Co-created report.** The expert and the client publish a report. The expert promotes it. Feeds Service 7 (content) and Service 14 (PR).
3. **Webinar guest.** The expert speaks at a client webinar and promotes it. Feeds Service 16.
4. **Thought leader ads.** The client runs the expert's post as a LinkedIn Thought Leader Ad, with the expert's written permission.

## 4. Audience match first

An agent scores every creator before a person sees the name. Score from 0 to 100:

| Factor | Weight | How measured |
|---|---|---|
| Audience country match | 25 | % of audience in the target market (tool data) |
| Audience age and gender match | 15 | Overlap with client persona |
| Interest and niche match | 15 | Topics of the last 30 posts |
| Engagement quality | 20 | Engagement rate against tier median, comment quality, real-follower % |
| Brand safety | 15 | No high-risk posts in 24 months, no competitor deal in 90 days |
| Content quality and fit | 10 | Agent review of the last 12 posts against the client voice |

Hard stops (score does not matter):
- Real-follower rate under 75% (tool estimate).
- Audience in the target market under 30% (B2C) or under 20% (B2B).
- Creator under 18, unless the client and a parent or guardian agree and the market allows it. Never for alcohol, gambling, finance or weight loss.
- A post in the last 24 months with hate, harassment, or illegal content.
- A creator who needs a licence in the market (UAE, Saudi Arabia) and has none.
- A paid deal with a named competitor in the last 90 days, or an active exclusivity.

## 5. Fee method

Pith M sets a fair fee from data, not from the creator's first ask.

1. Expected views = median views of the creator's last 10 non-sponsored posts on that platform.
2. Base fee = expected views ÷ 1,000 × the market CPM band for that platform and tier.
3. Add for usage rights: +30% to +100% of base for 3 to 12 months of paid use.
4. Add for exclusivity: +20% to +50% of base, by length and category width.
5. Add for extra deliverables (raw files, extra cuts, link in bio).
6. Offer the result as a range. The negotiation agent drafts; a person agrees the final number.

CPM bands per market start as estimates. After the first 10 closed deals in each market, replace them with Pith M's own median paid CPM. Keep the bands in the client brain so every client gains from the data.

## 6. Usage rights and exclusivity defaults

| Right | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Organic repost by the brand | 12 months, included | On brand-owned channels |
| Paid use (whitelisting, dark posts, Spark Ads, Partnership Ads) | 3 months, paid extra | Extend at a set price agreed in the contract |
| Edits | Cut-downs, captions, resize allowed. No change to the creator's words or meaning | Written approval for any other edit |
| Exclusivity | Category only, 30 days after the last post | Never a full ban on all brands |
| AI use | No AI voice or face clone of the creator | Only with separate written consent and a separate fee |
| Raw files | Not included | Price separately |

## 7. Brand-safety rules

- The client brain lists banned topics, banned words and competitors. The vetting agent checks the last 24 months of posts against them.
- A creator who posts a high-risk item during a live deal triggers a pause: no new posts go live until a person reviews.
- Every contract has a morals clause and a takedown clause.
- Content must not make a claim that is not on the approved claims list.

## 8. Fairness to creators

- Pay within 15 business days of proof of posting (target: 7).
- Pay the creator's local currency where the payout tool supports it.
- Plain-language contracts, in the creator's language where possible.
- No unpaid spec work. Gifting has no required post.
- Each creator gets their own results after the campaign.

These rules protect the client's name too. Creators talk to each other.
