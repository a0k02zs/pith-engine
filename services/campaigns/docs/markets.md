# Per-market campaign rules

Source: the country sections of the Pith M Automation Plan (draft 4, 21 Oct 2026). This is a planning summary, not legal advice. A local lawyer confirms each market before the first launch there. The compliance gate holds the machine-readable version of these rules.

## 1. United Kingdom (launch 1st)
- **Language and format:** British English, GBP, DD/MM/YYYY. Direct, low-hype tone.
- **Channel mix bias:** B2B LinkedIn + email + Google (+ Microsoft Ads). B2C Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Meta, Google Shopping. WhatsApp for service.
- **Key campaign windows:** January sales, Black Friday, Christmas, Boxing Day; B2B end of tax year (5 April).
- **Gate checks:** PECR consent or soft opt-in for email/SMS to individuals; corporate email allowed with identity and opt-out. CAP Code / ASA "Ad" label on paid creator posts. Cookie consent before tracking. Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 raises PECR fines.

## 2. Singapore (launch 2nd)
- **Language and format:** English; Mandarin, Malay, Tamil for some B2C. SGD, DD/MM/YYYY. Practical, value-led tone.
- **Channel mix bias:** B2B LinkedIn, email, Google. B2C Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp; Shopee and Lazada for e-commerce.
- **Key campaign windows:** Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Deepavali, 9.9 / 11.11 / 12.12, Great Singapore Sale.
- **Gate checks:** PDPA consent and purpose. Do Not Call Registry check before marketing SMS or calls. Spam Control Act: "<ADV>" label, sender details, unsubscribe on bulk email. ASAS disclosure for creator posts.

## 3. North America: United States and Canada (launch 3rd)
- **Language and format:** US English, USD, MM/DD/YYYY. Canadian English, CAD; French for Quebec (own creators and ads). 6 time zones: schedule sends per zone.
- **Channel mix bias:** B2B LinkedIn, email, Google + Microsoft Ads, podcasts, G2. B2C Instagram, TikTok (check status before each campaign), YouTube, Meta, Pinterest, Reddit, Amazon.
- **Key campaign windows:** Super Bowl, July 4, Canada Day (1 July), Labour Day, both Thanksgivings (different dates), Black Friday / Cyber Monday, Boxing Day (Canada), Q4 B2B budget season.
- **Gate checks:** US CAN-SPAM (postal address, opt-out within 10 business days), TCPA written consent for SMS, CCPA/CPRA and Global Privacy Control, FTC endorsement and fake-review rules, special ad categories. Canada CASL consent before first email or SMS, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, Charter of the French Language, Ad Standards disclosure.

## 4. GCC: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman (launch 4th, UAE and Saudi first)
- **Language and format:** English for most B2B; Gulf Arabic for B2C and government-linked buyers (right-to-left layouts). AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR. Weekend is Sat–Sun in the UAE, Fri–Sat in the other 5. Respectful, premium tone; more conservative in Saudi Arabia.
- **Channel mix bias:** WhatsApp for follow-up in all 6. B2C Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat (very strong in Saudi), X (Saudi, Kuwait), YouTube. B2B LinkedIn, email, events (GITEX, LEAP). Creators are very strong.
- **Key campaign windows:** Ramadan and Eid (dates move each year), Saudi Founding Day (22 Feb), Kuwait National Day (25 Feb), Saudi National Day (23 Sep), UAE National Day (2 Dec), Qatar National Day (18 Dec), Dubai Shopping Festival, White Friday.
- **Campaign rules:** during Ramadan, move ad spend and sends to evening and night hours; no launch in the last 10 days of Ramadan unless it is an Eid campaign. Never schedule on a local weekend for B2B.
- **Gate checks:** each country's data law (UAE PDPL plus DIFC/ADGM, Saudi PDPL with data-transfer limits, Qatar PDPPL, Bahrain PDPL, Oman PDPL, Kuwait CITRA). Registered SMS sender IDs and send-time limits per telecom regulator. Creator licence on file: UAE Media Council permit, Saudi Mawthooq. Content respects religion, culture and public morals.

## 5. European Union (launch 5th, 1 or 2 lead countries, Netherlands is the pipeline example)
- **Language and format:** English works for Dutch and Nordic B2B; German, French, Spanish need a native editor. EUR, DD/MM/YYYY or DD.MM.YYYY. Factual, precise tone.
- **Channel mix bias:** B2B LinkedIn, email, Google, trade events; XING in German-speaking markets. B2C Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Meta, WhatsApp; Bol.com, Zalando.
- **Gate checks:** GDPR lawful basis and Article 14 notice for third-party data; ePrivacy opt-in (double opt-in normal in Germany); cookie consent before tracking; Unfair Commercial Practices Directive and national creator laws (France 2023); EU AI Act Article 50 labels for AI-generated content (from 2 Aug 2026); Digital Services Act ad transparency.
