# Service 15 · Affiliate and partner marketing · Market rules

Version 1 · 21 Oct 2026. Launch order: UK, Singapore, North America, GCC, EU (`contracts/enums.json`, `market_launch_order`).

This is a planning summary from the plan's market sections. It is not legal advice. A local lawyer
confirms each market before launch. The compliance gate (`POST /compliance/check`) applies the
rules for the country of each partner and each buyer.

## Rules that apply in all 5 markets

1. Every affiliate and partner must disclose the commercial relationship in each post, page, email or
   video that has a tracked link or code. The label goes before the link, not only in a footer.
2. Partners must not make claims about the client's product that are not in the approved claims list
   in the client brain.
3. Partners must not bid on the client's brand keywords unless the terms allow it.
4. Partner outreach (recruitment) is marketing. It needs a lawful basis for the partner's country.
5. Payouts need tax details from the partner before the first payment.
6. Customer referral messages are sent by the customer, not by the client. The client may not send
   messages to the referred friend until that friend gives consent.

## Market notes for programme design

| Market | What works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| UK | Strong affiliate network market (Awin is large in the UK). Comparison and cashback sites are big. | Cashback and voucher sites take credit for sales that happen anyway. Pay them less. |
| Singapore | Shopee and Lazada affiliate programmes, creators, deal communities on Telegram. | Small market: recruit across Southeast Asia only if the client ships there. |
| US / Canada | Largest pool of content affiliates and SaaS partners. PartnerStack and Impact.com for B2B SaaS. | 1099 and W-8 paperwork. Quebec needs French partners. |
| GCC | Creators and WhatsApp communities sell. B2B: resellers and system integrators carry relationships. | Partner licences (UAE, Saudi Arabia). Ramadan changes the calendar and the buying peaks. |
| EU | Start with 1 or 2 lead countries (the Netherlands first, per the pipeline). Awin and Tradedoubler in Europe. | Translation by native editors. Strict consent for referral emails. |
