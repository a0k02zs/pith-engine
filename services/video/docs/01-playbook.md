# 01 · Video production playbook

## 1. Goal

Make many short videos fast for ads and social. Make fewer, high-quality videos for brand and sales.
Agents do scripts, rough cuts, captions, dubbing and resizing. People do filming, voice talent,
the final cut on hero videos, and every rights decision.

## 2. What the client buys

| Format | Length | Main use | B2B / B2C | Default track |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short-form social | 6–45 s | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn feed | Both | AI or UGC |
| Paid ad variants | 6, 15, 30 s | Meta, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Snapchat ads | Both | AI or UGC |
| Explainer | 60–120 s | Website, sales emails, landing pages | B2B first | AI (motion graphics) |
| Product demo | 30–180 s | Product pages, sales calls, onboarding | Both | AI (screen capture) or filmed |
| Customer testimonial | 30–120 s | Sales, ads, case studies | B2B first | Filmed or remote record |
| Founder / executive video | 30–90 s | LinkedIn, PR, recruiting | B2B | Filmed or remote record |
| Hero brand film | 30–90 s + cutdowns | Launches, campaigns, YouTube pre-roll | Both | Filmed |
| Webinar and event clips | 15–90 s | Social, email, follow-up | B2B | AI cut from recording |

## 3. The 3 production tracks

The pipeline is the same for every job. Only stage 6 (Produce) changes by track.

### Track A · AI-made
- Inputs: approved script, brand kit, stock library, client product images and screen captures.
- Methods: motion graphics, stock footage, product animation, AI voice, AI avatar (only with a
  signed likeness and voice consent, see `04-templates.md` T4).
- Best for: ad variants at volume, explainers, demos, localized versions.
- Turnaround target: 2–3 working days from approved script.
- Limits: no AI avatar of a real person without consent. No synthetic "customer" in a testimonial
  (see `05-market-rules.md`). Avoid AI faces for B2B trust content.

### Track B · Creator UGC
- Inputs: approved script or talking points, creator brief, product shipped to creator.
- Creators come from the influencer pipeline (Service 6) or a UGC marketplace.
- Best for: paid social ads, TikTok and Reels, B2C product proof.
- Turnaround target: 7–10 working days (product shipping is the main delay).
- Rules: contract must grant paid usage rights, term and markets. Creator discloses where they post
  on their own channel.

### Track C · Filmed shoot
- Inputs: approved script, shot list, call sheet, location and talent releases, permits.
- Crew: freelance producer, camera operator, editor. Remote-record kits for testimonials.
- Best for: hero films, testimonials, executive video, premium product video.
- Turnaround target: 10–15 working days from approved script.
- Rules: release from every identifiable person, location permit where the market needs one,
  drone permit where used.

### Track choice rule (the planner agent applies it; a person can override)
1. Real customer or employee speaks on camera → Track C (or remote record).
2. Paid social, B2C, need for authentic voice → Track B.
3. Volume variants, explainer, demo, localization of an existing video → Track A.
4. Hero or launch film → Track C, then Track A for cutdowns and variants.
5. Budget under the Track C floor → Track A or B, and the agent tells the account lead why.

## 4. B2B and B2C plays

### B2C
- Lead with hooks. Each ad gets 5 hooks on 1 body. Test hooks first, then bodies.
- Native look beats polished look on TikTok and Reels. Use Track B and lo-fi Track A.
- Build a creative "library": winning hooks, angles and formats per client, stored in the client brain.
- Refresh ads when the hook rate falls 25% from its first-week level, or frequency goes above 3 in 7 days.

### B2B
- Lead with proof: customer testimonials, demos, numbers.
- LinkedIn first: square or vertical, captions on, first frame states the problem.
- Every long video (webinar, testimonial, demo) is cut into 5–10 clips for social, email and sales.
- Sales enablement: a 60-second "what we do" video and 1 testimonial per industry per client.

## 5. Hook and script frameworks the agents use

- **Hook types:** problem call-out, bold claim (approved claims only), pattern break visual,
  question, "before and after", social proof number, POV, direct address to a role ("CFOs:").
- **Short-form body:** Hook (0–3 s) → Problem (3–8 s) → Proof or demo (8–20 s) → Offer → Call to action.
- **Explainer body:** Problem → Cost of the problem → How it works in 3 steps → Proof → Call to action.
- **Testimonial body:** Who I am → What was wrong before → Why we chose them → Result with a number → Advice.
- Captions burned in on every social and ad video. Most feed video plays with the sound off.

## 6. Packages (structure; Pith sets the prices)

| Package | Monthly output | Tracks | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 8 short videos + 3 sizes each | A | Small B2C brands, social only |
| Growth | 20 short videos, 40 ad variants, 2 UGC creators | A + B | B2C performance clients |
| B2B Proof | 2 testimonials or demos, 15 clips cut from them, 1 explainer per quarter | A + C | B2B clients |
| Hero | 1 filmed film per quarter + 30 cutdowns and variants | C + A | Launches and campaigns |
| Localization add-on | Captions and dub per extra language | A | Any package, per market |

Prices are not set in this build. The cost model in `06-kpis.md` gives the inputs to set them.

## 7. What the agents never decide alone

- Use a real person's face or voice (needs a signed consent record).
- Make a synthetic testimonial or a synthetic customer. This is never allowed.
- Publish a new product claim, price, health, finance or legal statement.
- Commit spend: crew, talent, creator fee, music licence, paid stock above the per-job limit.
- Publish a video that has not passed the final review gate.
