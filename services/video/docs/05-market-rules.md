# 05 · Per-market rules for video

These rules feed the compliance gate and the `compliance-precheck`, `localizer` and `qc-checker` agents.
**This is a planning summary, not legal advice.** A local lawyer confirms each market before launch,
and the compliance owner reviews this file every month (platform rules change often).

Launch order (from the master plan): United Kingdom → Singapore → North America → GCC → EU.

---

## Rules for every market

1. **Consent before likeness.** Get a signed release from every identifiable person. AI avatar and
   AI voice clone each need their own explicit consent (T4). Filming consent is not enough.
2. **No fake people as proof.** Never make a synthetic customer, a synthetic testimonial, or a
   review read by an actor as if it is a real customer. Actor demonstrations carry an on-screen
   "Actor portrayal" or the market equivalent.
3. **Claims.** Only approved claims. Results and numbers need evidence in the client brain.
4. **Creators and partners.** Every paid creator video carries the market's ad label inside the
   video or post, and the platform's paid-partnership tool where it exists.
5. **AI content labels.** Label realistic AI-generated or AI-altered people, voices or events.
   Keep content credentials (C2PA metadata) that tools add; do not strip them.
6. **Music and stock.** Only licensed libraries recorded in the licence log (T5). On TikTok business
   accounts, use only sounds from the Commercial Music Library.
7. **Accessibility.** Captions on every video. Sidecar captions (WebVTT) for web and sales video.
8. **Drones.** Only licensed operators with the market's aviation permits.

### Platform AI and ad labels (check every quarter)
| Platform | AI disclosure | Paid disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Creator must disclose realistic altered or synthetic content in the upload flow | "Includes paid promotion" setting |

## 1 · United Kingdom (launch 1st)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ad rules | CAP Code (non-broadcast) and BCAP Code (TV) enforced by the ASA. TV ads need clearance (Clearcast) before broadcast. |
| Creator disclosure | Label paid creator content "Ad" (or "Advert", "Advertising") at the start, inside the video or caption. |
| Filters and edits | Do not use beauty filters or edits that exaggerate the effect of a cosmetic or beauty product (ASA rulings since 2021). |
| AI | No AI-specific ad law; the CAP Code rules on misleading ads apply to AI content. Label realistic synthetic people and voices. |
| Personal data | UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018: images and voices of identifiable people are personal data. Use releases and a privacy notice. |
| Filming permits | Local council film office for public locations; private land needs owner permission. Drones: CAA Operator ID and Flyer ID. |
| Language and style | British English. Low-hype, dry tone. GBP. |
| Accessibility | Captions on all video; Equality Act 2010 for services. |

## 2 · Singapore (launch 2nd)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ad rules | Singapore Code of Advertising Practice (ASAS). Broadcast ads follow IMDA codes. |
| Creator disclosure | ASAS guidelines on interactive marketing: disclose paid or sponsored content clearly. |
| Personal data | PDPA 2012: consent and notified purpose for filming and using a person's image and voice in marketing. |
| AI | No AI ad label law; label realistic synthetic people. Do not make deepfakes of political candidates (election law amended in 2024). |
| Filming permits | Location owner permission; some public places need agency permits (for example parks or housing estates). Drones: CAAS permits. |
| Language and style | English; Mandarin, Malay or Tamil versions for some B2C audiences. Practical, value-led tone. SGD. |
| Timing | Avoid shoot days on major festivals (Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali); plan content for 9.9, 11.11, 12.12. |

## 3 · United States (launch 3rd, with Canada)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Endorsements | FTC Endorsement Guides: disclose material connections clearly in the video itself, not only in the description. |
| Testimonials | FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, in force since Oct 2024) bans fake testimonials, including AI-made ones from people who do not exist. |
| Claims | FTC substantiation: have evidence before you show a claim. Extra rules for health, finance and "Made in USA" claims. |
| Likeness and voice | State right-of-publicity laws. Some states have laws on AI voice and digital replicas (for example Tennessee's ELVIS Act, and California rules on digital replica contract terms). Union talent: SAG-AFTRA commercial contract terms for digital replicas. |
| Drones | FAA Part 107 certified pilot. |
| Accessibility | Captions; ADA claims about web accessibility are common, so add WebVTT captions on site video. |
| Language and style | US English, MM/DD/YYYY, USD. Confident, benefit-first, with proof. Spanish versions for Hispanic audiences where the brief asks. |

## 4 · Canada (launch 3rd, with the US)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ad rules | Competition Act (deceptive marketing; since 2024, environmental claims need proof under the Act's tests). Canadian Code of Advertising Standards. |
| Creator disclosure | Ad Standards Influencer Marketing Disclosure Guidelines: clear label such as #ad or #sponsored in the video and the post. |
| Quebec | Charter of the French Language: marketing in Quebec must be in French; another language may appear only as permitted (French at least as prominent). Make a French master, not only French captions, and cast French-speaking creators. |
| Personal data | PIPEDA; Quebec Law 25. Law 25 has extra duties for biometric data, so check with counsel before any voice or face clone of a Quebec resident. |
| Accessibility | AODA (Ontario) web standard for larger organisations: captions on web video. |
| Drones | Transport Canada drone pilot certificate. |
| Language and style | Canadian English and French. CAD. |

## 5 · Middle East · GCC (launch 4th: UAE and Saudi Arabia first)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Content standards | Respect religion, national identity, rulers and public morals. No alcohol, pork, gambling or dating content. Modest dress. No mockery of the state, rulers or religion. Do not use national emblems, flags or images of rulers without permission. Saudi Arabia is more conservative than the UAE. |
| Filming people | UAE: filming or photographing a person without consent can be a criminal offence (cybercrime law, Federal Decree-Law 34 of 2021). Get a written release from everyone identifiable, including passers-by. Avoid crowds in public shots or blur faces. |
| Filming permits | UAE: film permits from the emirate film authority (for example Dubai Film and TV Commission; Abu Dhabi film authority). Saudi Arabia: filming permit from the national film or media authority. Drones: aviation authority and local permits; permits are strict. |
| Creators | UAE: Media Council advertiser permit for anyone who advertises on social media. Saudi Arabia: Mawthooq licence from GCAM. Check rules in Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman per campaign. |
| Personal data | UAE PDPL (separate laws in DIFC and ADGM); Saudi PDPL (limits on sending personal data out of the country; check before uploading footage of Saudi residents to a non-Saudi cloud tool); Qatar PDPPL; Bahrain PDPL; Oman PDPL; Kuwait data privacy regulation. |
| AI | Label realistic synthetic people. Saudi SDAIA has published guidance on deepfakes; check the current version before any AI likeness work. |
| Language and style | Gulf Arabic voice-over for B2C and government-linked buyers; English for most B2B. Right-to-left captions and end cards. Premium, respectful tone. |
| Timing | Ramadan: change tone, posting times and shoot schedules; Eid content. Weekends: Saturday–Sunday in the UAE, Friday–Saturday in the other 5. National days (UAE 2 Dec, Saudi 23 Sep, Qatar 18 Dec, Kuwait 25 Feb). |

## 6 · European Union (launch 5th; start with 1–2 lead countries, for example the Netherlands)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| AI Act Article 50 | From 2 August 2026, anyone who uses AI to make a deepfake (realistic image, audio or video of a person, object or event) must disclose that it is AI-made or AI-altered. Label it on screen. Keep machine-readable markings that the AI tool adds. |
| Personal data | GDPR: image and voice are personal data; get consent and give a privacy notice. Treat face and voice models as sensitive data; do a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) before any clone. |
| Hidden ads | Unfair Commercial Practices Directive bans hidden advertising; label paid content. |
| France | Influencer law (2023): label "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale"; label "Image retouchée" for retouched images and "Image virtuelle" for AI-made faces or bodies. Toubon law: advertising in France must be in French. |
| Influencer registers | Some countries apply media rules to large influencers (for example the Netherlands media authority registers creators above a follower threshold). Check per lead country. |
| Accessibility | European Accessibility Act (applies from 28 June 2025) covers e-commerce services; add captions to product and service video. |
| Language and style | Native editor for every non-English master. Factual, precise tone. EUR. DD/MM/YYYY or DD.MM.YYYY. |

| Singapore | "Sponsored" / "Ad" | "AI-generated" | – |
| US | "Ad" / "Sponsored" / "Paid partnership" | "AI-generated" | "Actor portrayal" for actors |
| Canada | "#ad" / "#sponsored"; French "#pub" / "#commandité" in Quebec | "Généré par IA" in Quebec | French master in Quebec |
| GCC | "إعلان" (Advertisement) and "Ad" | "مُنشأ بالذكاء الاصطناعي" (made with AI) and English | Creator licence number where required |
| EU | Local-language "Ad" label; France "Publicité" / "Collaboration commerciale" | Local-language AI label; France "Image virtuelle" | France "Image retouchée" |

Arabic and French labels: a native reviewer confirms the exact wording per client before launch.
