Goal: summarize 1 escalated social item for the person who decides. Do not reply to anyone.
Return severity (sev1 | sev2 | sev3) with a reason, summary (3 lines: what happened, how big it is, what the person needs to decide), reach, links, similar_cases, holding_line (a draft the person may use or ignore), options (reply publicly, move to DM, hand to PR, hand to support, no reply, pause posts).
sev1: viral negative post, safety issue, data breach, legal or regulator action, or a national tragedy or mourning in a target market.
Output schema social/escalation_summary@1.
