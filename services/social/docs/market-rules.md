# Market rules for organic social

Launch order (plan): United Kingdom, Singapore, North America, GCC, European Union. The checker agent reads `market-rules.json`, which holds the same rules as data. If this file and the JSON differ, the JSON wins and the difference is a bug.

This is a planning summary. It is not legal advice. A local lawyer confirms each market before launch.

## Rules for every market

- Paid or gifted collaboration posts carry the platform's own disclosure tool (Instagram and Facebook "Paid partnership", TikTok "Branded content" toggle, YouTube "Includes paid promotion", LinkedIn "Brand partnership") **and** a clear label in the text that fits the market.
- Employees who post about the client on their own accounts disclose that they work for the client.
- Realistic AI-generated or AI-altered images, video or audio carry the platform AI label. In the EU, also follow EU AI Act Article 50 (from 2 August 2026).
- Every image has alt text. Every video has captions. This is good practice everywhere and a legal duty for some clients (for example under the European Accessibility Act).
- Business accounts use only commercially licensed music and sounds.
- Personal data from comments and DMs: keep the minimum, use it only to answer, delete it after 90 days unless the person becomes a contact through the engine data layer with consent.
- Contests and giveaways: written terms, platform promotion rules, and local prize law. Every contest is a Gate B item.

## 1 · United Kingdom (launch 1st)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | British English spelling. Direct, low-hype tone; understatement over big claims |
| Platforms | B2B: LinkedIn. B2C: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook for over-45s |
| Disclosure | CAP Code and ASA: label paid creator posts "Ad" at the start, not hidden in hashtags. Gifted-only posts: "Gifted" is not enough if the brand controls content; use "Ad" |
| Claims | CAP Code: evidence before any objective claim. Green claims follow the CMA Green Claims Code |
| Restricted sectors | Alcohol, gambling, financial promotions (FCA rules apply to social posts, including memes) and HFSS food (check paid online ad restrictions before boosting any post). These sectors: every post is Gate B |
| Key dates | January sales, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day (UK date in March), Easter, Black Friday, Christmas, Boxing Day; B2B: end of tax year (5 April) |
| Sensitive | Remembrance period (November): no promotional use of the poppy |

## 2 · Singapore (launch 2nd)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | English. Add Mandarin, Malay or Tamil for some B2C audiences (native editor). Practical, value-led tone |
| Platforms | B2B: LinkedIn. B2C: Instagram, Facebook (still strong), TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp Channels; Telegram for some communities |
| Disclosure | ASAS Code and its guidelines on interactive marketing and influencer marketing: disclose paid posts clearly |
| Data | PDPA 2012: consent and a notified purpose for any personal data from comments, DMs or contests |

| Key dates | Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji, Deepavali, National Day (9 August), 9.9, 11.11, 12.12 sales, Great Singapore Sale |

## 3 · North America (launch 3rd)

### United States

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | US English. Confident, benefit-first tone with proof |
| Platforms | B2B: LinkedIn, X for some sectors. B2C: Instagram, TikTok (check the current platform situation before each month), YouTube, Pinterest, Facebook, Reddit (community rules first) |
| Disclosure | FTC Endorsement Guides: "#ad", "Sponsored" or "Paid partnership" visible without a click. In video, say it as well as show it |
| Reviews and influence | FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (2024): no fake reviews, no buying or selling fake followers or views |
| Children | COPPA: no data collection from children under 13. No content directed at children unless the client is set up for it |
| Time zones | Schedule for the audience: ET and PT at minimum for national accounts |
| Key dates | Super Bowl, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Q4 budget season (B2B) |

### Canada

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | Canadian English spelling. **Quebec: French is required** (Charter of the French Language). Quebec posts are written by a native French editor, not translated by the agent alone |

| Key dates | Canada Day (1 July), Canadian Thanksgiving (October), Boxing Day, Black Friday |

## 4 · Middle East, GCC (launch 4th: UAE and Saudi Arabia first)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | English for most B2B. Gulf Arabic for B2C and government-linked buyers, written by a native editor. Right-to-left layouts for Arabic graphics |
| Tone | Respectful, premium, relationship-first. Saudi Arabia is more conservative |
| Platforms | B2B: LinkedIn. B2C: Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat (very strong in Saudi Arabia), X (strong in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait), YouTube. WhatsApp Channels for broadcast |
| Week | Weekend Saturday–Sunday in the UAE; Friday–Saturday in the other 5 countries. No posts at Friday prayer time (about 12:00–14:00 local Friday) |
| Influencer licence | UAE: Media Council advertiser permit for anyone who posts paid content. Saudi Arabia: Mawthooq licence. Check the licence before any collab post |
| Content | Respect religion, culture and public morals in all 6 countries. No alcohol, pork, gambling, romantic or revealing imagery. Modest dress in imagery for Saudi Arabia. No political content. No maps that show disputed borders |
| Legal risk in replies | Cybercrime and media laws can make defamation or insulting content online a criminal matter. Never name, shame or argue in public. Move the issue to private chat and to a person |
| Ramadan | Lower volume in daytime, no food or drink imagery in daytime posts in Saudi Arabia, warm and giving tone, posting peak after iftar. Ramadan and Eid dates move each year: the planner reads them from the date table |
| Key dates | Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Saudi Founding Day (22 February), Kuwait National Day (25 February), Saudi National Day (23 September), UAE National Day (2 December), Qatar National Day (18 December), Dubai Shopping Festival, White Friday |
| National days | Use official colours and emblems only as the rules allow. National-day posts are Gate B |

## 5 · European Union (launch 5th: Netherlands first)

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Language | English works for Dutch and Nordic B2B. German, French and Spanish need a native editor. Factual, precise tone; avoid US-style hype |
| Platforms | B2B: LinkedIn; XING in German-speaking markets. B2C: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, WhatsApp Channels |
| Disclosure | Unfair Commercial Practices Directive and national law. France: influencer law of 2023 (clear "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale" label, AI image label). Netherlands: Media Act rules; influencers above the Commissariaat voor de Media threshold must register |
| AI labels | EU AI Act Article 50 from 2 August 2026: label AI-generated or manipulated content (deepfakes) |
| Germany | Business social profiles need a link to an Impressum (legal notice) |
| Data | GDPR for comment, DM and contest data. Contest entry data needs a privacy notice |
| Platform law | Digital Services Act: follow platform notice and appeal processes for content takedowns |
| Key dates | Per country. Netherlands: King's Day (27 April), Sinterklaas (5 December); plus Black Friday, Christmas |
